About the Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz

The Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz is a fun, fictional survival decision-making game.

You are presented with different situations during a fictional zombie outbreak and must decide what you would do. Your choices determine your final survival score.

How It Works

The quiz contains 20 questions, with four possible choices for each situation. Each choice awards a different number of points.

At the end of the quiz, your score is converted into a survival category ranging from Zombie Food to The Last Human Alive.

Understanding Your Score

Your final score is based on the decisions you make throughout the 20-question survival scenario. Some choices prioritize immediate safety, while others focus on preparation, resources, teamwork, and long-term survival.

The maximum possible score is 100. Your result places you into one of five fictional survival categories, ranging from Zombie Food to The Last Human Alive.

What the Quiz Tests

The scenarios are designed around common survival themes such as decision-making under pressure, managing limited resources, choosing safe routes, evaluating unfamiliar people, protecting shelter, and planning for the future.

There isn't a single correct strategy for every fictional situation. The quiz is designed to make you think about the consequences of different choices while having fun.

Just for Fun

This quiz is fictional entertainment and is not intended to provide real-world emergency, medical, security, or survival advice.

Challenge your friends and see who would survive the longest!
