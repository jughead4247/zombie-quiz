About the Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz

The Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz is a fun, fictional survival decision-making game.

You are presented with different situations during a fictional zombie outbreak and must decide what you would do. Your choices determine your final survival score.

How It Works

The quiz contains 20 questions, with four possible choices for each situation. Each choice awards a different number of points.

At the end of the quiz, your score is converted into a survival category ranging from Zombie Food to The Last Human Alive.

Just for Fun

This quiz is fictional entertainment and is not intended to provide real-world emergency, medical, security, or survival advice.

Challenge your friends and see who would survive the longest!
