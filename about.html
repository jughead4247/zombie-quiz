About the Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz

The Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz is a fictional decision-making experience designed to answer one question: How long would you survive a zombie apocalypse?

You will face a series of fictional situations involving shelter, food, water, resources, dangerous encounters, other survivors, and difficult decisions.

How It Works

The quiz contains 20 questions. Each situation presents four possible choices.

Every choice has a different survival value. Your decisions are added together to produce your final survival score.

Your Survival Score

Your final score ranges from 0 to 100 points. The score represents how effectively your decisions performed within the fictional scenarios presented by the quiz.

Your choices may test your ability to manage resources, find shelter, assess danger, make difficult decisions, cooperate with other survivors, and respond to unexpected situations.

Estimated Survival

After completing all 20 questions, your score is converted into a fictional estimated survival time.

The result may range from surviving only a short time during the outbreak to becoming one of the last survivors remaining.

There Isn't Always One Correct Choice

The quiz is designed around difficult decisions rather than obvious right and wrong answers.

Some choices may be safer but less useful in the long term. Other choices may provide greater rewards while exposing you to additional risks.

Your final result reflects the overall pattern of decisions you make throughout the quiz.

A Fictional Scenario

The zombie outbreak used in this quiz is entirely fictional. The situations and results are created for entertainment and should not be treated as predictions of real-world behavior.

Challenge Your Friends

Once you finish the quiz, compare your result with your friends and see who can survive the longest.

Just for Fun

The Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz is an entertainment experience. It is not a professional survival, emergency, medical, psychological, military, or personality assessment.
