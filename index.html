About the Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz

The Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz is a 20-question fictional survival challenge that asks how you would respond if a zombie outbreak suddenly changed the world around you. Instead of testing zombie trivia, the quiz puts you into difficult situations where your decisions affect your chances of surviving.

The scenarios cover the early stages of an outbreak, shelter, food and water, resources, movement, injuries, other survivors and the challenges of finding a more permanent safe location.

Some choices may appear attractive in the moment but create larger problems later. Other decisions reward preparation, observation, resource management and thinking beyond the immediate danger.

The quiz is designed for entertainment and fictional decision-making. Your result estimates how well your choices perform within the scenarios presented by the quiz; it is not a scientific measurement of real-world survival ability.

How the Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz Works

The quiz contains 20 fictional survival situations. Every question has four possible choices, but the choices can have different point values depending on how useful the decision would be within the scenario.

The highest-value decisions generally balance safety, preparation, information, resources and long-term sustainability. Lower-value decisions may involve unnecessary risks, poor preparation or choices that create additional problems later.

The maximum possible score is 100 points. At the end of the quiz, your score is used to place you into one of five fictional survival categories, ranging from Zombie Food to The Last Human Alive.

The result is intended as a game-style assessment. There is no scientifically accurate way to predict survival in a fictional zombie outbreak, so your result should be treated as entertainment rather than real emergency advice.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first survival situation will appear with four possible choices.

Selecting an answer

Read the situation and all four choices before making your decision. Select the option you believe gives you the best chance of surviving that particular scenario.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier situation and change a previous decision before submitting the quiz.

Finishing the quiz

Continue until you have completed all 20 situations. Submit the quiz when you are finished to see your final score, survival category and estimated survival result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After receiving your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and experiment with different decisions. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to compare your survival results with other players.

Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 20 fictional survival situations, and each question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum possible score is 100 points. Your final score determines which fictional survival category you receive.

What kinds of situations are included?

The quiz includes situations involving shelter, resources, food, water, movement, injuries, other survivors, risk assessment and long-term survival.

Are the zombies and outbreak real?

No. The zombie outbreak is completely fictional. The quiz is designed as an entertainment and decision-making game.

Why are some choices worth more points?

Each choice has a different point value based on how useful that decision would generally be within the fictional situation presented in the quiz.

Can I change my answers?

Yes. You can use the Back button to return to earlier questions and change your decisions before submitting the quiz.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the quiz and make different survival choices.

Can I share my result?

Yes. After completing the quiz, you can use SHARE MY RESULT to share your score and survival category. You can also challenge your friends to see who scores higher.

Is this a real survival test?

No. The result is fictional and intended for entertainment. It does not measure physical fitness, intelligence, professional emergency training or real-world survival ability.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz may contain spoilers for the fictional zombie-apocalypse scenarios presented throughout the quiz. Some questions reveal situations, dangers, decisions and possible survival outcomes.

If you prefer to experience each scenario without seeing its details in advance, start the quiz before reading further into the questions or discussing your results with other players.
