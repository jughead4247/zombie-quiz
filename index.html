About the Zombie Apocalypse Survival Quiz

Think you could survive a zombie apocalypse? Put your decision-making skills to the test in this fictional survival quiz.

You'll face 20 situations covering food, water, shelter, resources, other survivors, and dangerous encounters. Every choice affects your final score.

Your score ranges from 0 to 100 and determines your fictional survival ranking, from Zombie Food to The Last Human Alive.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Zombie Survival Quiz work?

You will face 20 fictional zombie-apocalypse situations. Each situation gives you four choices, and every choice awards a different number of points. Your decisions determine your final survival score.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum possible score is 100 points. Your final score determines which survival category you receive.

What does my survival score mean?

Your result is a fictional estimate based on the choices you made. Scores range from Zombie Food at the lower end to The Last Human Alive at the highest level.

Is this a real survival test?

No. The quiz is designed for entertainment and does not predict how someone would actually perform during a disaster or zombie outbreak.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result and you can replay the quiz and try different decisions.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the Challenge Your Friends button to share your result and invite your friends to see if they can beat your score.
